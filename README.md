# Camera Lighting Profiles — feature guide

- Scene List: Save camera views as thumbnail scenes and switch by clicking anywhere on a scene row.
- Update scene: Replace a saved scene with the current viewport view.
- Camera inspector: Adjust position, rotation, aperture, exposure, lens, sensor, crop and focus.
- Perspective exit: Leave a scene camera and return to Unreal's normal perspective viewport.
- One-click render: Render a saved camera using the selected template and output settings.
- Render templates: Choose Will-based Lumen or Path Tracer presets and adjust the useful settings directly.
- Full MRQ editor: Open the complete native Movie Render Queue preset for advanced configuration.
- Batch queue: Queue stills and clips, select jobs, and apply shared render settings before rendering.
- Clips timeline: Arrange camera shots, drag their edges, set exact times and zoom with Ctrl+wheel.
- Camera drag and drop: Drop camera actors from the World Outliner onto the timeline.
- Viewport recording: Record navigation into a new editable camera take at your chosen frame rate.
- Interpolation: Choose linear, smooth or constant camera movement between recorded views.
- Native Sequencer export: Open the generated camera animation in Unreal's Sequencer.
- Composition guides: Overlay thirds, golden ratio, diagonals, spirals, horizons and cinema framing guides.
- Draft rendering: Produce lightweight previews before committing to final-quality frames.
- Render comparison: Compare rendered images with an A/B viewer and exposure adjustment.
- Preflight: Check camera snapshots, renderer readiness, sampling, visibility, storage and output paths.
- Frame recovery: Render missing or damaged frames while retaining valid images and frozen job settings.
- Video delivery: Select MP4 or ProRes MOV directly in Clips and render/encode automatically with optional audio and FPS.
- Render history: Keep completed outputs, errors and separate full-render attempts accessible.
- Output formats: Save PNG, JPEG or EXR image sequences for delivery or further processing.
- Panel recovery: Re-register missing plugin panels after editor layout changes.
